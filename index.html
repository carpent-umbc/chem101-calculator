2. Regular Exams & Cumulative Final

60% of Course Grade
Why Raw Exam Average Differs from Exams 1–3 (Option 1 vs. Option 2)

Your Exams 1–3 (which include exam bonus points) are ranked from highest to lowest for 45% of your course grade: highest = 20%, middle = 15%, and lowest = 10%.

For the remaining 15%, Option 1 uses your Cumulative Final Exam score, whereas Option 2 uses your Raw Exam Average (the unweighted average of your raw exam scores without exam bonus points), which is listed directly in your Blackboard grades.

3. Bonus Opportunities

Up to +1.20% Course Boost
What is the Exploration Bonus? (From Syllabus) You can complete Explorations to earn bonus points toward your Unit Checkpoint score. For each Exploration completed with a score of 95% or higher, +1% point is added to your Unit Checkpoint average (which adds +0.05% to your overall course grade, up to +0.20% across all four).
  • Where to find them: Offered only in Units 1, 4, 6, and 8 in REAL CHEM.
  • Attempts & Deadline: You have 5 attempts for each Exploration. Due December 9th at 11:59 PM.
