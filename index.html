How this estimate is calculated

Course Weighting:

  • Module Completion (10%): Modules 1–19 = 9.05% (19/21), Module 20 = 0.48% (1/21), Module 21 = 0.48% (1/21).
  • Unit Checkpoints (5%): Units 1–7 = 4.38% (7/8), Unit 8 = 0.63% (1/8).
  • Unit Completion (5%): Units 1–6 = 3.75% (6/8), Unit 7 = 0.63% (1/8), Unit 8 = 0.63% (1/8).
  • Discussion (20%): Direct 20% category weight.
  • Regular Exams (45%): Automatically ranked from highest to lowest: highest = 20%, middle = 15%, lowest = 10%.
  • Final-Exam Component (15%): Uses your official Raw Exam Average if you do not take the final, or your cumulative final score if you take it.
  • Bonuses: Added directly to the overall course percentage (+0.05% per Exploration, +0.50% per Survey).
