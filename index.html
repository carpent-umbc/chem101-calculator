How Coursework is Calculated & Auto-Estimated

Coursework makes up 40% of your final grade: Modules 1–21 (10% total, ~0.48% each), Unit Checkpoints 1–8 (5% total, 0.63% each), Unit Completions 1–8 (5% total, 0.63% each), and Discussion (20%). If you leave upcoming items (Modules 19–21, Checkpoint 8, or Units 7–8) blank, the calculator automatically fills them with your average from the earlier items in that category.

2. Regular Exams & Cumulative Final

60% of Course Grade
How Exam Ranking & Option 1 vs. Option 2 Work

Your 3 regular exams (45% total) are automatically sorted from highest to lowest: your highest exam counts 20%, your middle exam counts 15%, and your lowest exam counts 10%. For the remaining 15%, Option 1 uses your Cumulative Final Exam score, whereas Option 2 replaces the Final Exam with your raw unweighted average across Exams 1–3.

3. Bonus Opportunities

Up to +1.20% Direct Boost
How Bonus Points Add to Your Grade

Bonus items add directly onto your final course percentage in both Option 1 and Option 2. Completing the Pre-Survey or Post-Survey earns +0.50% each (5 pts × 10% weight), and each Unit Climate/Energy bonus earns +0.05% (1 pt × 5% weight).
